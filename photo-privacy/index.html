Open this page over HTTP

This tool loads ES modules, which browsers block when a page is opened straight from disk over file://. Nothing below would run.

From the project folder, run npm run dev and open http://localhost:5500/photo-privacy/.

Photo Privacy

See what a photo gives away — where, when, which camera — and remove it

Everything runs in your browser. Your photos are never uploaded.
Drop photos here or
JPEG, PNG, WebP and iPhone HEIC

JPEG, PNG and WebP are cleaned without recompressing: the picture is copied byte for byte and keeps its colour profile. HEIC photos come back as JPEG.
